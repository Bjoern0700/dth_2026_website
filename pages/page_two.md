# More of open source

## Disadvantages of open source

The disadvantages in open source appear in even having a big community caring for the construct of open source and making it as secure.


### Examples of disadvantages

1. Insertion of unnoticed changes

There is always a chance for someone to sneak in changes or features, which do not belong in the project/s trying to sabotage them, steal data or manipulating in any sort of other ways. And that also can happen because of the big variety of people having access to the projects, because one never truly knows, what intention the joining user might have. And there might never be an absolute certainty, that the intention of any user either in generally is negative or becomes negative over time. Also, as Alex Carter describes the way of bug has a significance as well, because one issue is to integrate an error in a system (Carter, 2024). 

2. Disguising flaws

Another and perhaps far more important flaw is, how this can be done. Some users might be excellent at disguising their flaws into the system, that it either takes a lot of time and energy or worse, that it never is found (Chen et. Al., 2025, 11648-11649). And depending on what the program, the flaw was merged into contains, and what the bug does, it might slow down or steal or manipulate important data (Nord, Ozkaya, 2017 and Shull, 68-69). Here it also makes it difficult to determine who is responsible for the error due to both the open accessibility for users and for the fact that a username might be used by multiple people making it more difficult finding the root of the problem. Because when communicating with a person thinking they are from a specific place and later finding out they might have taken a username from another person to disguise themselves, might take time to discover who it actually is.  One thing having in mind as well is, that the concept of “free” in this sense only refers to the user’s currency, which the software converts into funding their data (AlMarzouq et. Al., 778).

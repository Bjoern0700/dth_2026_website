# Open source

## Advantages of open source

Open source is a possibility for all people to edit websites in a large degree without having to worry about if it is allowed or not. 


### Examples of advantages

- Transparency, safety and payment

Errors can be found much more easily rapid and efficient, because the range of people having access to them is a lot larger, than if there was a limit on people being able or allowed to work on them. Programmers do not need much of approval from anywhere and can do their work. Rather than being dependent on the owner of the software, open source relies on licenses, which users can acquire to use the software without the owner having anything to do with the project. Security also benefits, because of the transparent user’s privacy and personal data within large corporations and governments. 
Open source also is free to use for everybody, which micht make it more considerable to use than closed source. One thing having in mind as well is, that the concept of “free” in this sense only refers to the user’s currency, which the software converts into funding their data.



- Modification of workflow

There are chances to modify one’s own workflows within the desired field of work, whether it being a work project, something of own interest, etc. Having something work on open source also makes more certain of it being present over time and not vanishing at some point, because of the original creator does not alter in the project anymore. It can be continued by the community that might have caught the projects interest, so it is not going to stop abruptly. 

- *Learning and network building*

The learning and network building also benefits from open source, because people can help each other both faster and with more solutions, because there are more possibilities of perspectives on open source projects due to the wider range of the chain. Accompanied to this, the trustworthiness is perhaps a positive point, when having a community of people working together on varieties of projects and contribute to them and help each other improving their ideas and thoughts to the best digital versions. Also regarding to market share on open source being free might be advantaged, because there simply are more people available to share ideas or projects, when the interests are matching.
